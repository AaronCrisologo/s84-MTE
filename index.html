About LearnLoop

LearnLoop started as a shared spreadsheet between classmates who were tired of recommending the same five links. It grew into a small, maintained catalogue of free learning material — organised by subject, checked by humans, and open to everyone.

We are not a school and we do not sell courses. We are a signpost: a short, opinionated list of places that actually teach well.

Our mission

To make high-quality learning free, findable and uncluttered — so that curiosity is the only thing you need to bring.

  • Free. Every listing costs nothing to use.
  • Honest. No affiliate links or paid placements.
  • Open. Corrections and suggestions always welcome.
  • Accessible. Keyboard-first, screen-reader friendly.
